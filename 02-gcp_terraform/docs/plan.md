# Terraform Masterclass for GCP FDEs

This training path is designed for experienced GCP Engineers to master Terraform. It focuses on the transition from imperative interaction to declarative, stateful infrastructure-as-code (IaC).

## High-Level Roadmap
1. **Module 1: Foundations & GCP Mental Model** - State, Backends, and basic resource mapping.
2. **Module 2: Advanced Modular Design** - Scaling to multiple environments/projects.
3. **Module 3: Complex Orchestration** - Meta-arguments, IAM at scale, and dependencies.
4. **Module 4: Enterprise Day-2 Ops** - Drift management, importing, and CI/CD.
